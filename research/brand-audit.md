# Brand and claim audit

## Overall

The site presents a clear, evidence-led identity: an economist whose theory and computation on money and the non-observed economy feed directly into Israeli policy analysis. Copy is in the third person throughout, because no first-person text was supplied or approved. It avoids rank inflation, metrics and awards. Interpretive lines (hero statement, research lede, pillar framing) are flagged for approval.

## Audience clarity

| Audience | Can they find it within one click? |
|---|---|
| Researchers | Yes. Home pillars and selected papers lead to paper pages with DOI and citation, and the full bibliography has filters. |
| Policymakers / journalists | Yes. Home policy cards and the Policy page link to Taub pages and PDFs. The contact email is on every page footer. |
| Organisers | Yes. Home and Media link to About#bios, which has copyable short and long bios. |
| Students | Partly. Institutions, guidebooks and open resources are shown, but no course pages exist yet. |

## SEO copy

Every page has a distinct `<title>` and description. Paper pages carry Highwire `citation_*` tags (title, every author, dates, journal, volume, issue, pages, DOI) and `ScholarlyArticle`/`Chapter` JSON-LD. `citation_pdf_url` is deliberately omitted because no PDFs are hosted. Home and About carry `Person` JSON-LD with verified `sameAs` profiles only.
