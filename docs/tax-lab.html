Academic research initiative

Israel Tax Research Lab

Research on taxation, compliance, and the non-observed economy in Israel.

01 About the Lab

Understanding how taxation shapes the economy

The Israel Tax Research Lab is an academic platform for research on Israel’s tax system and the non-observed economy. It brings together early-career researchers, doctoral candidates, and master’s students to examine how tax policy and administration shape economic activity, compliance, public revenues, and society.

The Lab conducts rigorous research on tax policy and its effects, compliance and tax evasion, tax administration and enforcement, money laundering, illicit financial flows, and economic activity not fully captured in official statistics.

02 Research agenda

Areas of focus

The Lab studies the institutions, incentives, and economic activity connected to taxation.

Tax policy and its effects

How tax design affects households, businesses, employment, investment, inequality, and public revenue.

Compliance and tax evasion

Why people and firms comply with tax rules or evade them, and how incentives, norms, and enforcement shape those choices.

Tax administration and enforcement

How tax authorities measure compliance, address the tax gap, and design effective and proportionate enforcement.

The non-observed economy

How to measure economic activity that is not fully recorded, understand its drivers, and assess its fiscal and wider economic implications.

Money laundering and illicit financial flows

Connections between financial opacity, tax-related offences, money laundering, and the movement of illicit funds.

04 A collaborative academic community

Research across disciplines and career stages

The Lab brings together early-career researchers, doctoral candidates, and master’s students in a collaborative academic community. Its work includes joint research, working papers, seminars, and policy-focused knowledge exchange.

Researchers and students interested in these fields are invited to contact the Lab.

05 Knowledge exchange

Research partnerships

The Lab collaborates with the Israel Tax Authority and the Budget Department of Israel’s Ministry of Finance. These partnerships connect academic research with policy priorities and support evidence-based work on taxation, compliance, tax evasion, and public revenue.
