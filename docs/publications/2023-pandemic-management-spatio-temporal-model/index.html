Pandemic management by a spatio–temporal mathematical model

Teddy Lazebnik, Svetlana Bunimovich-Mendrazitsky and Labib Shami

International Journal of Nonlinear Sciences and Numerical Simulation 24(6), 2307–2324

Read the article

Summary

A spatio-temporal epidemic model that accounts for disease severity and for heterogeneous contacts across age and susceptibility, used to design and evaluate strategies for managing the COVID-19 pandemic in three countries.

This summary was written for this website from the published abstract. The publisher’s abstract and full text are the authoritative versions. No full text is reproduced here.
