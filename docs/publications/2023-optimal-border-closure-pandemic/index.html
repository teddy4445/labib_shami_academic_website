Journal article · 2023

A hybrid mathematical model for an optimal border closure policy during a pandemic

Teddy Lazebnik, Labib Shami and Svetlana Bunimovich-Mendrazitsky

International Journal of Applied Mathematics and Computer Science 33(4) Open access

Read the article

Summary

A hybrid epidemiological and economic model, with an artificial-intelligence component, to help decision-makers choose border-closure policies during a health crisis that limit an outbreak while protecting tourism revenue.

This summary was written for this website from the published abstract. The publisher’s abstract and full text are the authoritative versions. No full text is reproduced here.
