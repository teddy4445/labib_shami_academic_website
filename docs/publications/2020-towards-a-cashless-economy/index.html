Journal article · 2020

Towards a cashless economy: Economic and socio-political implications

Nissim Cohen, Anna Rubinchik and Labib Shami

European Journal of Political Economy 61, 101820

Read the article

Summary

A general-equilibrium model of an economy with official and informal sectors, showing how eliminating cash can misallocate resources and reduce the gains from trade, alongside other unintended consequences of abolishing cash.

This summary was written for this website from the published abstract. The publisher’s abstract and full text are the authoritative versions. No full text is reproduced here.
