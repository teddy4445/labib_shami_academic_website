The circular, step by step - three core domains, social entrepreneurship model

Limor Yehuda, Sagit Barel-Shaked, Labib Shami, Gil Cohen, Osnat Akirav and Arie Herscovici

International Journal of Entrepreneurship and Small Business 51(4), 433–452

Read the article

Summary

Uses a case study of an organisation working to reduce agricultural crime to identify the core domains of social enterprises and to formulate a working model of social entrepreneurship.

This summary was written for this website from the published abstract. The publisher’s abstract and full text are the authoritative versions. No full text is reproduced here.
