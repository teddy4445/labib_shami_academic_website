Farmers' typology in an environment of policy reform

Sagit Barel-Shaked, Labib Shami, Arie Herscovici, Osnat Akirav, Gil Cohen and Limor Yehuda

International Journal of Entrepreneurship and Small Business 52(1), 116–130

Read the article

Summary

Builds a typology of the strategies Israeli egg farmers adopt as a policy reform threatens their economic viability, identifying contrasting farmer identities, including farmers driven mainly by non-economic goals.

This summary was written for this website from the published abstract. The publisher’s abstract and full text are the authoritative versions. No full text is reproduced here.
