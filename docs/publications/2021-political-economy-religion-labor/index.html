Book chapter · 2021

The Political Economy of Religion and Labor

Labib Shami and Osnat Akirav

Handbook of Labor, Human Resources and Population Economics (ed. Klaus F. Zimmermann), Springer, 1–24 Book chapter

Read the article

Summary

Brings economics and political science together to examine how religious actors gain political influence and use it to shape labour-market policy and their followers' work behaviour, with Israel's ultra-Orthodox community as a central case.

This summary was written for this website from the published abstract. The publisher’s abstract and full text are the authoritative versions. No full text is reproduced here.
