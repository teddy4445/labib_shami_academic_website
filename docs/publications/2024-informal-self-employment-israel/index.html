Journal article · 2024

Informal self-employment within the non-observed economy of Israel

Labib Shami, Gil Cohen, Osnat Akirav, Arie Herscovici, Limor Yehuda and Sagit Barel-Shaked

International Journal of Entrepreneurship and Small Business 52(2), 159–180

Read the article

Summary

Adapts a currency-demand approach to the Israeli economy to estimate the size of the non-observed economy and the share that comes from informal activity by the self-employed, finding a downward trend over the preceding decade.

This summary was written for this website from the published abstract. The publisher’s abstract and full text are the authoritative versions. No full text is reproduced here.
