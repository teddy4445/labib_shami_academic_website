Journal article · 2024

The technological incubators program as a growth generator of peripheral areas in Israel

Gil Cohen, Limor Yehuda, Sagit Barel-Shaked, Labib Shami, Arie Herscovici and Osnat Akirav

International Journal of Entrepreneurship and Small Business 51(1), 120–136

Read the article

Summary

Examines Israel's technological incubator programme in peripheral regions through a value-chain lens. The main challenge it identifies is building a local ecosystem, with mentoring partners and access to university or hospital facilities, that supports ventures during and after incubation.

This summary was written for this website from the published abstract. The publisher’s abstract and full text are the authoritative versions. No full text is reproduced here.
