Journal article · 2022

Spatio-Temporal influence of Non-Pharmaceutical interventions policies on pandemic dynamics and the economy: the case of COVID-19

Teddy Lazebnik, Labib Shami and Svetlana Bunimovich-Mendrazitsky

Economic Research-Ekonomska Istraživanja 35(1), 1833–1861 Open access

Read the article

Summary

A graph-based spatio-temporal SIR model that jointly assesses how non-pharmaceutical interventions, such as the length of working and school days and lockdowns, affect both pandemic spread and economic losses, calibrated with Israeli data.

This summary was written for this website from the published abstract. The publisher’s abstract and full text are the authoritative versions. No full text is reproduced here.
