Journal article · 2024

Microfounded Tax Revenue Forecast Model with Heterogeneous Population and Genetic Algorithm Approach

Ariel Alexi, Teddy Lazebnik and Labib Shami

Computational Economics 63(5), 1705–1734

Read the article

Summary

An agent-based model of consumers and businesses with heterogeneous attributes, whose decisions evolve through a genetic algorithm, used to forecast local tax revenues and evaluate taxation policies. It is tested against a small village's recorded tax data.

This summary was written for this website from the published abstract. The publisher’s abstract and full text are the authoritative versions. No full text is reproduced here.
