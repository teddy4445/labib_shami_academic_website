Policy

Policy analysis

At the Taub Center for Social Policy Studies in Israel, Dr. Labib Shami studies Israel’s macroeconomy and the economic pressures on households. Since 2021 he has co-authored, with Prof. Benjamin Bental, the macroeconomic chapter of the Center’s annual State of the Nation Report.

Cost of living, housing & household finance

16 September 2026

The Consumer Price Index, Housing Costs, Consumer Credit, and the Cost of Living

Benjamin Bental and Labib Shami

Argues that Israel's consumer price index understates the cost pressure on households because it leaves out mortgage and consumer-credit interest, constructs an alternative finance-based housing measure, and recommends publishing supplementary indices alongside the official one.

Owning or Renting? The Dynamics of Housing Tenure in Israel

Benjamin Bental and Labib Shami

Follows the same Israeli households from 2013 to 2022 to trace movements between owning, renting and living in family-owned homes, with particular attention to rising homeownership in Arab society and to international comparisons.

29 November 2023

Interest, Inflation, and Mortgages in Israel

Labib Shami, Ori Oberman and Benjamin Bental

Simulates how Bank of Israel rate increases, compared with inflation left unchecked, would change households' monthly mortgage payments, and concludes that unchecked inflation would hurt borrowers more than the rate increases.

10 February 2019

Household Debt in Israel

Labib Shami

Breaks down Israeli household debt by income decile, age group and population group, finding a debt-to-GDP ratio that is low by international standards but rising, with particular vulnerability among the poorest households.
