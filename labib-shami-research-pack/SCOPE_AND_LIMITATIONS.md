# Scope and known limitations

The public web was used to verify institutional identities, scholarly references, public Taub Center reports and a diverse set of media links. This package is an **editorial research inventory**, not the subject's officially approved complete CV, not a paywalled article download, and not a licensed photo collection. Dates and publication statuses require refreshed verification before launch.

Sources include publisher DOI pages, Taub Center and Haifa University institutional pages, the 2021 WGC-hosted CV, the official Taub videos, radio episode pages, academic bibliography databases, podcasts, and press articles. No copied full media articles, photos, audio, publisher PDFs or restricted document text are embedded.

Do not quote research findings numerically without checking original published papers, their methodology and dates. No current citation totals or performance/award claims are offered as website content. All candidate titles and bios need subject approval.
