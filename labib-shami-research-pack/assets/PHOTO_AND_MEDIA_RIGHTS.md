# Visual assets — source discovery vs permission

The portrait URLs in `asset_manifest.csv` are discovery references only. **They are NOT included as licensed images.** Ask the person for an approved high-resolution portrait (preferably landscape and vertical crops), image credit and clear website rights.

Reuse conditions vary:

- Institutionally hosted portrait: website access does not establish reuse permission.
- ResearchGate avatar: thumbnail quality is inadequate and redistribution rights are unknown.
- Taub Center research figures: include linked reports; only republish a graphic if license or written permission allows it.
- YouTube: use normal embeds according to platform settings; do not download and republish video.
- Podcast/radio: link to the original player and embed only when the original provider explicitly supplies compliant embed code.
- Press/news: link and summarize in original words; avoid copying article body or images.
- Logos: institutional branding should be used only in a factual affiliation context, consistent with institution guidelines.

Suggested alternatives when photos are unavailable: restrained typographic hero, custom abstract vector diagram, reconstructed charts using properly licensed public data, or portrait placeholder visibly marked for content review (not on the deployed public homepage).
