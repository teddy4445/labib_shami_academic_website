How monetary arrangements, consumer credit and inflation shape the opportunities and risks experienced across an economy.

**The economy beyond official statistics**  
Models of tax evasion, informal employment, and non-observed transactions, including computational methods for difficult-to-measure activity.

**Policy, entrepreneurship and resilience**  
Evidence and models for small-business performance, local policy, housing and macroeconomic challenges, especially in Israel.
