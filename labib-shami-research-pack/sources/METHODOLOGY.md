# Research and verification methodology (2026-10-10)

## Identity resolution

Started from Taub Center and University of Haifa institutional biographies. Cross-checked university-hosted CV, college event profile, ORCID from a PubMed article and publisher pages. Excluded people with overlapping names.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Current official institutional profile for professional position and email.
2. Publisher/DOI/Crossref bibliographic record for paper, venue, license and published date.
3. Direct publisher video/audio or verified organizer for talks and interviews.
4. ResearchGate and academic search engines as a discovery aid only; deduplicate and check metadata.
5. Old CVs for historical degrees/appointments only.

## Special caution

University of Haifa portal reported 36 research outputs when accessed, including a report; ResearchGate reported 37 entries including duplicates, preprints and online-first items. Both totals can change. This pack contains 28 manually curated scholarly items and 13 candidate Taub Center report landing pages. **This is a working bibliography, not a completeness guarantee.**

DOI-backed paper links route to official landing pages. Preprint and book chapter data need human review. The asset manifest has URLs but no redistribution permissions. Linked editorial descriptions are original concise summaries rather than copied publisher abstracts.

## Rights labels

`link_only`: citation and hyperlink are okay; copying files/figures/text requires separate permission.  
`permission_required`: do not copy into the static site without license or written permission.  
`license_verified`: no content in this current pack was certified license-verified for mirroring.

## Refresh procedure

Before deployment recheck recent 2026 works, DOI metadata, policy reports and official titles; add a `verified_at` date. Confirm institutional claims with Dr. Shami and don't automatically build full-text article pages from third-party abstracts.
