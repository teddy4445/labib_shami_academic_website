## Longer (third person, web-ready after sign-off)

Dr. Labib Shami researches the economic processes that influence households, businesses, and public policy. He serves as a Senior Researcher at the Taub Center for Social Policy Studies in Israel, works with the Department of Economics at Western Galilee College, and teaches economics at the University of Haifa. He earned a PhD in Economics from the University of Haifa in 2018.

His scholarship addresses macroeconomics, monetary policy, shadow and informal economic activity, tax policy, and the role of economic modeling in understanding institutional choices. Alongside peer-reviewed articles, he contributes policy studies on issues including housing, inflation, the cost of living, household credit, small businesses, and macroeconomic developments in Israel. Some collaborative work extends into machine learning, agent-based simulation, and the economics of crises.
