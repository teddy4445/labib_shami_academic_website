Teaching

Teaching

Labib Shami teaches economics at Western Galilee College and has taught in the Department of Economics at the University of Haifa. He has published two Hebrew-language guidebooks for students of finance.

01 Institutions

Where he teaches

  • Western Galilee CollegeDepartment of Economics, Department of Management and the MA programme in Economics and ManagementLecturer, and student advisor in the Department of Management. Also listed among the faculty of the College’s English-taught BA in Economics and Management.
  • University of HaifaDepartment of Economics, Faculty of Social SciencesTaught as a Teaching Associate.

Open resources from this work
