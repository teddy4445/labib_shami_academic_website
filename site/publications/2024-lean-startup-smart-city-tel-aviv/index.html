Lean startup as a model for smart city projects: the case of Tel Aviv-Yafo

Arie Herscovici, Osnat Akirav, Gil Cohen, Limor Yehuda, Sagit Barel-Shaked and Labib Shami

International Journal of Entrepreneurship and Small Business 53(2), 245–257

Read the article

Summary

Argues that cities adopting information and communication technology also adopt management methods from the technology industry, illustrated by the lean-startup approach in Tel Aviv-Yafo's smart city project.
