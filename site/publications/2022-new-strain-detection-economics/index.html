Journal article · 2022

Economic aspects of the detection of new strains in a multi-strain epidemiological–mathematical model

Labib Shami and Teddy Lazebnik

Chaos, Solitons & Fractals 165, 112823

Read the article

Summary

Uses an epidemiological, genomic and economic model to study testing policies for detecting new pathogen strains. A well-designed genomic-sequencing policy can be both economically and epidemiologically efficient, while random population sampling gives sub-optimal results.

This summary was written for this website from the published abstract. The publisher’s abstract and full text are the authoritative versions. No full text is reproduced here.
