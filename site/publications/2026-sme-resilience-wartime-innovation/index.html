SME resilience in wartime: the constraints of innovation

Labib Shami and Israel Rachevski

Innovation: Organization & Management, 1–18 Online first

Read the article

Summary

Draws on a survey of 766 Israeli SMEs affected by the 2023 war to examine how firm characteristics, geographic exposure and wartime constraints relate to supply- and demand-side disruptions in the first phases of the conflict, and whether process and marketing innovation moderate those relationships.

This summary was written for this website from the published abstract. The publisher’s abstract and full text are the authoritative versions. No full text is reproduced here.
