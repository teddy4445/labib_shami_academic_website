Beyond the First Impression: A Two-Period Screening Model under Probationary Employment

Dan Lederman, Teddy Lazebnik and Labib Shami

SSRN working paper Preprint

Read on SSRN

Summary

A two-period model of probationary employment in which workers can either build genuine skill or improve the metrics an employer observes. It examines how pay brackets and evaluation windows shape effort, and includes an application to professional basketball (NBA) contracts.

This summary was written for this website from the published abstract. The publisher’s abstract and full text are the authoritative versions. No full text is reproduced here.
