Journal article · 2023

Academic co-authorship is a risky game

Teddy Lazebnik, Stephan Beck and Labib Shami

Scientometrics 128(12), 6495–6507 Open access

Read the article Preprint (arXiv:2207.13170)

Summary

A game-theoretic model of co-authored research in which an author can issue an ultimatum, blocking publication unless given more credit. It explores how contributions, project duration and stage, and team size affect the incentive to do so.

This summary was written for this website from the published abstract. The publisher’s abstract and full text are the authoritative versions. No full text is reproduced here.
